> An AI-powered strategic preparation platform for the UPSC Civil Services Examination (CSE) and Combined Defence Services (CDS), featuring 2,528+ solved Prelims Previous Year Questions (2011–2025), interactive GIS Mapping & Places in News (PIN), and syllabus-linked current affairs.

- [UPSC Prelims PYQ Explorer (2011–2025)](https://www.upscgpt.in/prelims/pyq): Complete archive of 2,528+ authentic UPSC Civil Services (CSE) and CDS Prelims Previous Year Questions organized by Subject and Topic, with verified official UPSC answer keys and detailed conceptual explanations.
- [Interactive Geography & Places in News (PIN) Workspace](https://www.upscgpt.in/mapping): Curated UPSC Geography Atlas covering National Parks, Tiger Reserves, Ramsar Wetlands, River Systems, Mountain Passes, and Strategic Locations linked directly to UPSC Prelims PYQs.

- [Mains Command Center & Neural Base](https://www.upscgpt.in/mains/prepare): Consolidated Mains study guides and topic-wise conceptual notes.
- [Daily Current Affairs & News Streaks](https://www.upscgpt.in/news): Longitudinal current affairs timelines mapped to UPSC Prelims and Mains syllabus themes.
