          About the data

          —

          • UPSC CSE Prelims questions only. Subjects and themes come from the Neon database. Sub-topics and the current-affairs flag were tagged for the 2011–2023 questions; for the others they are estimated by a Naive Bayes model trained on those tags (the CA flag is right about 4 times in 5). Theme notes (what to study, repeated concepts) were written from the 2011–2023 papers.
          • Trend compares questions per paper in 2021–26 with 2011–15: rising or falling means at least 1.5× and half a question apart. Under 4 questions is "Too few".
          • Orange-edged question cards are representative of their theme. Some answers are marked tentative in the source.
