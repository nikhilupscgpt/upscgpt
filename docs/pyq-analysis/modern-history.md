### 1.5 High-Yield Recurring Concepts (Subject-Wide)
- Charter Acts (1793, 1813, 1833, 1853) and Government of India Acts (1858, 1909, 1919, 1935)
- Gandhian mass movements (Non-Cooperation, Civil Disobedience, Quit India) — participants, parallel governments, and pacts
- Socio-religious reformers, newspapers/journals, and educational commissions (Wood’s Despatch, Hunter, Sadler, Macaulay)
- Peasant, tribal, and working-class movements (Santhal, Munda/Ulgulan, Indigo, Pabna, Deccan Riots, Tebhaga, Factory Acts)
- Economic critique of colonialism (Dadabhai Naoroji, R.C. Dutt), land revenue systems (Permanent, Ryotwari, Mahalwari)

### 1.6 Signature UPSC Traps & Elimination Pitfalls
- **Trap:** Swapping provisions between GOI Act 1919 (Dyarchy in Provinces) and GOI Act 1935 (Provincial Autonomy + Dyarchy at Centre, residuary powers with Governor-General).
- **Trap:** Associating a newspaper, book, or sabha with the wrong contemporary reformer (e.g., Bengal vs Maharashtra reformers).
- **Trap:** Chronological traps mixing events of 1905–1909 (Swadeshi, Surat Split, Morley-Minto) or 1940–1946 (August Offer, Cripps, Wavell, Cabinet Mission).

### 1.7 2026 Prelims Focus Predictions
- **2026 Watch:** Centenary/milestone clusters: 1924–1926 events (Belgaum Congress, Vaikom Satyagraha, Kanpur Bolshevik Conspiracy, Swaraj Party).
- **2026 Watch:** Constitutional & judicial milestones under Company/Crown rule (Lex Loci Act, Ilbert Bill, Whitley Commission, Lee Commission).
- **2026 Watch:** Lesser-known women freedom fighters, revolutionary networks abroad (Ghadar, Berlin Committee, INA trials), and tribal forest satyagrahas.
