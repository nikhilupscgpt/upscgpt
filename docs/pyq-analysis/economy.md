### 1.5 High-Yield Recurring Concepts (Subject-Wide)
- RBI monetary policy tools (LAF, Repo, SDF, MSF, CRR, SLR, OMO) and money multiplier determinants
- Inflation dynamics (CPI vs WPI weightage, core inflation, imported inflation, bond yield relationship)
- Balance of Payments (Current vs Capital account components, NEER/REER, external debt composition, forex reserves)
- Fiscal deficit, primary deficit, FRBM glide path, capital vs revenue receipts/expenditure
- Digital public infrastructure, NBFC/Urban Co-operative Bank regulation, and sovereign/corporate debt instruments

### 1.6 Signature UPSC Traps & Elimination Pitfalls
- **Trap:** Reversing bond price and bond yield directions when interest rates or inflation expectations change.
- **Trap:** Treating disinvestment receipts, loan recoveries, or RBI dividend transfers in the wrong budget account (Capital vs Revenue).
- **Trap:** Assuming "steadily increased/decreased over the last decade" for GDP growth, tax-to-GDP, or fiscal deficit trends.
- **Trap:** Confusing demand-pull vs cost-push liquidity effects of sterilization and Open Market Operations.

### 1.7 2026 Prelims Focus Predictions
- **2026 Watch:** CBDC (e-Rupee) programmable offline features, tokenization, and RBI Unified Lending Interface (ULI) vs UPI architecture.
- **2026 Watch:** Inclusion of Indian Government Bonds (FAR) in global bond indices — impact on Capital Account, rupee yield curve, and forex sterilization.
- **2026 Watch:**  T+0 settlement cycle, Alternative Investment Funds (AIFs), InvITs/REITs taxation, and sovereign green/thematic bonds.
- **2026 Watch:** Carbon border adjustments (CBAM), WTO agricultural subsidies (Peace Clause / Amber Box), and Free Trade Agreement rules of origin.
