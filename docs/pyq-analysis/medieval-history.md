### 1.5 High-Yield Recurring Concepts (Subject-Wide)
- Vijayanagara Empire: Krishnadevaraya’s reign, Amara-nayaka system, Mahanavami Dibba, waterworks, and foreign travellers (Abdur Razzaq, Domingo Paes, Nuniz, Nicolo Conti)
- Delhi Sultanate administrative departments (Diwan-i-Arz, Diwan-i-Kohi, Diwan-i-Bandagan, Diwan-i-Khairat) and market/land reforms of Alauddin Khalji & Muhammad bin Tughlaq
- Mughal Mansabdari (Zat & Sawar), Zabti/Dahsala revenue system, jagirdari crisis, and provincial officers (Faujdar, Amil, Kotwal, Mir Bakshi)
- Early Medieval tripartite struggle (Palas, Pratiharas, Rashtrakutas), Chola Uttaramerur sabha committees, and Kakatiya/Hoysala ports

### 1.6 Signature UPSC Traps & Elimination Pitfalls
- **Trap:** Treating Iqta or Jagir as hereditary private property by default rather than transferable revenue assignments.
- **Trap:** Swapping Delhi Sultanate departments between Balban, Alauddin Khalji, Muhammad bin Tughlaq, and Firoz Shah Tughlaq.
- **Trap:** Mismatching foreign travellers with the Vijayanagara or Mughal ruler whose court they visited.

### 1.7 2026 Prelims Focus Predictions
- **2026 Watch:** Ahom kingdom administration (Paik system, Buranjis, Moidams), Deccan Sultanates, and Maratha Ashtapradhan / Chauth-Sardeshmukhi.
- **2026 Watch:** Medieval agrarian & hydraulic terms (Araghatta/Persian wheel, Karez, Karkhanas, Hundi, Sarais) and Indo-Islamic coins.
- **2026 Watch:** Regional dynasties (Kakatiyas of Warangal/Motupalli, Hoysalas, Gajapatis of Odisha, Karkota/Utpala of Kashmir).
