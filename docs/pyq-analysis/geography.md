### 1.5 High-Yield Recurring Concepts (Subject-Wide)
- Monsoon mechanism, IOD, El Niño/La Niña, Westerly Jet Streams, and tropical cyclone formation conditions
- Himalayan vs Peninsular drainage systems, East-flowing vs West-flowing rivers, gorges, and tributaries
- Global heat budget, insolation, temperature inversion, cloud types, and ocean salinity/currents
- Indian soil types (Black, Laterite, Alluvial, Red), natural vegetation belts, and critical mineral belts
- Chokepoints, straits, seas, border countries, and conflict/resource zones in world geography

### 1.6 Signature UPSC Traps & Elimination Pitfalls
- **Trap:** Swapping river bank directions (left-bank vs right-bank tributaries) or source hills in the Eastern/Western Ghats.
- **Trap:** Confusing tropical vs temperate cyclone characteristics (frontal origin, eye formation, landfall dissipation).
- **Trap:** Misidentifying critical mineral reserves vs top producer states in India (Lithium, Cobalt, Rare Earths, Monazite, Bauxite).
- **Trap:** Reversing insolation/moisture relationships (e.g., relative humidity vs temperature changes, equatorial vs subtropical salinity).

### 1.7 2026 Prelims Focus Predictions
- **2026 Watch:** Critical & Strategic Minerals (30 listed minerals, offshore polymetallic nodules, Lithium/REE belts in J&K, Rajasthan, Karnataka).
- **2026 Watch:** Red Sea–Bab-el-Mandeb–Hormuz–Eastern Mediterranean maritime corridors and Arctic Northern Sea Route.
- **2026 Watch:** Glacial Lake Outburst Floods (GLOFs), Teesta/Chenab/Subansiri basin hydro-geography, and Western Disturbances anomalies.
- **2026 Watch:** Atmospheric rivers, sudden stratospheric warming, heat domes, and Deep Ocean Mission hydrothermal vents.
