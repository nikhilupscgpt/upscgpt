### 1.5 High-Yield Recurring Concepts (Subject-Wide)
- Buddhist & Jaina sects (Theravada, Mahayana, Vajrayana; Svetambara, Digambara), councils, canonical texts, and bodhisattvas
- Rock-cut caves (Ajanta, Ellora, Elephanta, Barabar, Nasik, Udayagiri-Khandagiri) and stupa/vihara architecture
- Temple architecture styles (Nagara sub-schools, Dravida Chola/Pallava/Vijayanagara, Vesara Hoysala/Chalukya) and sculptures
- Six orthodox (Astika) philosophical schools (Samkhya, Yoga, Nyaya, Vaisheshika, Mimamsa, Vedanta) and Bhakti/Sufi saints
- Classical & folk dances, musical traditions (Hindustani vs Carnatic), miniature paintings, and ancient Sanskrit/Pali/Prakrit plays

### 1.6 Signature UPSC Traps & Elimination Pitfalls
- **Trap:** Swapping Jain and Buddhist texts/concepts (e.g., Sthanakvasi, Paramitas, Sautrantika, Sammatiya,Nayaputta).
- **Trap:** Attributing a rock-cut monument or temple to the wrong dynasty or century (e.g., Kailashanatha Ellora vs Mahabalipuram vs Tanjore).
- **Trap:** Confusing contemporaneous Bhakti/Sufi saints with Mughal emperors or regional rulers.

### 1.7 2026 Prelims Focus Predictions
- **2026 Watch:** Newly inscribed UNESCO World Heritage Sites (Moidams of Assam, Hoysala ensembles) and Classical Language status criteria (Marathi, Pali, Prakrit, Assamese, Bengali).
- **2026 Watch:** Buddhist & Jaina epigraphy, sangha monastic rules (Vinaya Pitaka), and philosophical debates (Madhyamaka vs Yogachara, Syadvada).
- **2026 Watch:** Chola/Pallava/Chalukya port towns, temple guild inscriptions, and bronze/stone sculptural iconography.
