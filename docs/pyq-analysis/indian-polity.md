### 1.5 High-Yield Recurring Concepts (Subject-Wide)
- Scope and enforceability of Fundamental Rights (Articles 14, 19, 21) vs DPSP and Fundamental Duties
- Parliamentary devices, money bills vs financial bills, joint sitting exceptions, and committee mandates
- Discretionary powers of President and Governor, ordinance-making limits, and pardoning powers
- Supreme Court original vs appellate jurisdiction, judicial review, Basic Structure doctrine, and writ contours
- Fifth & Sixth Schedules, 73rd/74th Amendments, and asymmetric federalism provisions

### 1.6 Signature UPSC Traps & Elimination Pitfalls
- **Trap:** Swapping "Constitution explicitly defines/mentions" with "regulated by Parliamentary statute or convention" (e.g., office of profit, judicial review grounds, no-confidence motion).
- **Trap:** Confusing "all members of Parliament" with "elected members only" in electoral colleges vs impeachment/removal processes.
- **Trap:** Assuming DPSPs or Fundamental Duties are enforceable by writs, or that Constitutional Amendment Bills can go to a Joint Sitting.
- **Trap:** Over-generalising exclusive Rajya Sabha powers (Article 249, 312) vs Lok Sabha dominance in financial matters.

### 1.7 2026 Prelims Focus Predictions
- **2026 Watch:** Delimitation & Women Reservation implementation mechanics (106th Amendment) and census-linked seat freezes.
- **2026 Watch:** Governor reservation of bills (Articles 200/201), Pocket Veto vs Supreme Court timelines, and Speaker disqualification jurisdiction (10th Schedule).
- **2026 Watch:** Sixth Schedule autonomous councils, PESA operational rules, and sub-categorisation within Scheduled Castes.
- **2026 Watch:** Parliamentary privileges, Ethics Committee vs Privileges Committee, and Sovereign Green Bonds / Public Account custody.
