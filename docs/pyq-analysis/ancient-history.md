### 1.5 High-Yield Recurring Concepts (Subject-Wide)
- Indus Valley Civilisation: site-specific archaeological finds (Dholavira water harnessing, Lothal dockyard, Kalibangan, Rakhigarhi)
- Ashokan Major & Minor Rock Edicts, pillar inscriptions, Dhamma officers (Rajukas, Dhamma Mahamattas), and Mauryan economy
- Sangam literature (Tolkappiyam, Silappadikaram, Manimekalai, Ettuthokai), tinai eco-zones, and Indo-Roman ports (Muziris, Korkai, Arikamedu)
- Administrative, revenue, and guild terms (Vishti, Bhaga, Shreni, Eripatti, Ghatika, Agrahara) across Maurya–Gupta–Harsha eras

### 1.6 Signature UPSC Traps & Elimination Pitfalls
- **Trap:** Assuming Harappans used iron, horse-drawn chariots, or monumental stone temples.
- **Trap:** Confusing administrative/judicial terms across dynasties (e.g., Mauryan vs Gupta vs Chola village assemblies and land classifications).
- **Trap:** Misplacing ancient ports between the Malabar (West) and Coromandel (East) coasts.

### 1.7 2026 Prelims Focus Predictions
- **2026 Watch:** Harappan DNA/archaeobotanical findings (Rakhigarhi, Vadnagar, Karanpura) and Chalcolithic/Megalithic cultures.
- **2026 Watch:** Ashokan edicts site-to-state mapping (Sannati/Kanganahalli, Dhauli, Jaugada, Girnar, Erragudi) and Prakrit/Brahmi palaeography.
- **2026 Watch:** Sangam & Post-Mauryan maritime trade (Periplus of the Erythraean Sea ports, Roman coinage hoards, Satavahana/Kushana guilds).
