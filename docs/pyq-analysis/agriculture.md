### 1.5 High-Yield Recurring Concepts (Subject-Wide)
- Climatic & soil requirements of Kharif, Rabi, and Zaid crops (Millets/Shree Anna, Pulses, Oilseeds, Cotton, Sugarcane, Rice, Tea)
- Sustainable practices: Zero-tillage, Conservation Agriculture, System of Rice Intensification (SRI), Direct Seeded Rice (DSR), Fertigation, Mulching
- Nitrogen Use Efficiency, Nano-Urea/DAP, Neem-coated urea, bio-fertilizers (Rhizobium, Azolla, Azotobacter, Mycorrhiza), and green manuring
- MSP calculation (A2+FL vs C2), CACP recommendations vs CCEA approval, e-NAM, FPOs, and edible oil/pulse import dependence

### 1.6 Signature UPSC Traps & Elimination Pitfalls
- **Trap:** Assuming CACP approves MSP (CACP only recommends; Cabinet Committee on Economic Affairs approves) or that MSP is legally mandatory for private traders.
- **Trap:** Confusing Kharif vs Rabi classification of pulses/oilseeds (e.g., Tur/Arhar vs Gram/Chana, Groundnut vs Mustard).
- **Trap:** Overstating that SRI or DSR eliminates methane or water need completely rather than reducing it substantially.

### 1.7 2026 Prelims Focus Predictions
- **2026 Watch:** Oilseeds & Pulses self-reliance missions (Yellow Revolution 2.0, Mustard/Palm oil agro-climatic zones, Tur/Masoor buffer procurement).
- **2026 Watch:** Climate-resilient crop varieties, genome-edited crops (SDN-1/SDN-2 exemption rules), and digital crop survey (AgriStack).
- **2026 Watch:** Soil organic carbon sequestration, biochar, precision micro-irrigation, and millet value-chain processing.
