### 1.5 High-Yield Recurring Concepts (Subject-Wide)
- Genetic engineering: CRISPR-Cas9, RNAi, mRNA vaccines, mitochondrial replacement, CAR-T cell therapy, stem cells
- Space technology: LEO/MEO/GEO/GTO/SSO/Lagrange orbits, PSLV vs GSLV/LVM3 cryogenic stages, ISRO & global missions
- Communication & computing: 5G/6G spectrum, Li-Fi, NFC, RFID, Blockchain, Quantum Key Distribution, Web 3.0, AI/LLMs
- Disease biology: Viral vs bacterial vs protozoan pathogens, antimicrobial resistance, Wolbachia, T-cell/B-cell immunity
- Energy & materials: Fuel cells (Hydrogen/SOFC), Lithium-ion vs Sodium-ion batteries, small modular reactors, metamaterials

### 1.6 Signature UPSC Traps & Elimination Pitfalls
- **Trap:** In "can it be used for X, Y, Z?" application questions on emerging tech, narrow exclusions are usually false — broad multi-domain applicability is the norm unless physically impossible.
- **Trap:** Confusing DNA vs RNA viral genomes, or bacterial vs viral targets for antibiotics and vaccines.
- **Trap:** Mixing up fission vs fusion fuels (Thorium-232 fertile vs Uranium-233 fissile in India’s 3-stage nuclear programme).
- **Trap:** Confusing geostationary (equatorial plane, 35,786 km) vs geosynchronous / sun-synchronous polar orbits.

### 1.7 2026 Prelims Focus Predictions
- **2026 Watch:** Gene therapy & sickle-cell editing (Casgevy / indigenous CAR-T), mRNA therapeutics beyond COVID, and One Health AMR surveillance.
- **2026 Watch:** Gaganyaan human-rating, Bharatiya Antariksh Station, Aditya-L1 / LUPEX / NISAR radar bands, and semi-cryogenic engines.
- **2026 Watch:** Quantum computing (National Quantum Mission qubits, QKD), Small Language Models / Neuromorphic chips, and Deepfakes provenance.
- **2026 Watch:** Green Hydrogen electrolyzers (PEM vs Alkaline), Sodium-ion/Solid-state batteries, and Thorium-based Advanced Heavy Water Reactors.
