### 1.5 High-Yield Recurring Concepts (Subject-Wide)
- IUCN status, habitat specificity, and behavioural traits of Indian flagship and endemic species
- National Parks, Tiger Reserves, Ramsar Wetlands, and Biosphere Reserves paired with rivers and vegetation
- Wildlife (Protection) Act 1972 (2022 rationalised schedules), EPA 1986, Forest Rights Act 2006, and Biodiversity Act
- Global conventions & funds: UNFCCC, CBD (Kunming-Montreal GBF), Ramsar, CITES, CMS, Stockholm/Minamata/Basel
- Biogeochemical cycles (Nitrogen, Carbon, Phosphorus), trophic dynamics, and emerging pollutants (PFAS, microplastics, fly ash)

### 1.6 Signature UPSC Traps & Elimination Pitfalls
- **Trap:** Extreme words like "found ONLY in India" or "naturally found in no other state" — verify endemic range carefully.
- **Trap:** Confusing statutory parent acts of bodies (e.g., NBWL & NTCA under WPA 1972 vs Animal Welfare Board under PCA 1960 vs NGT).
- **Trap:** Mixing up UNFCCC mechanisms/funds (GEF, GCF, Adaptation Fund, Loss & Damage Fund) and whether India is a party/signatory.
- **Trap:** Swapping Nitrogen-fixing organisms, mycorrhizal associations, or primary vs secondary air pollutants.

### 1.7 2026 Prelims Focus Predictions
- **2026 Watch:** Carbon Credit Trading Scheme (CCTS) under Energy Conservation Amendment Act, Green Credit Rules, and Article 6 carbon markets.
- **2026 Watch:** WPA 1972 (2022 Amendment) 4-schedule structure (Schedules I, II, III-Plants, IV-CITES) and captive elephant transfer rules.
- **2026 Watch:** Newly notified Ramsar sites, 58th Tiger Reserve (Madhav TR), ECo-Sensitive Zones (ESZ) Supreme Court rulings, and grassland birds.
- **2026 Watch:** Methane abatement (Global Methane Pledge, coal-bed/rice-paddy emissions), blue carbon ecosystems (MISHTI), and e-waste EPR rules.
