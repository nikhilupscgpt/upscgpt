## 2. The 8-Part Pedagogical Architecture

Every question deconstruction strictly follows this sequence:

1. **Header & Question Stem**: Original question with `Exam`, `Year`, `Question Number`, `Source Base`, and `Topic Hierarchy`.
2. **Correct Answer Banner (Green)**: The unambiguous official UPSC answer key with full option text and 1-sentence `coreTakeaway`.
3. **Keywords & Concept Cards (Blue)**: **Strictly Lexical Rule**—only genuine core syllabus concepts, Constitutional Articles, Acts, or physical entities present in the question stem or options. **No synthetic meta-labels (e.g., no "Majoritarian Trap" or synthetic distraction tags).**
   - `term`: Clean noun/phrase shown on the question card pill (e.g., `"Article 394"`, `"Montreux Record"`, `"Sterilization"`).
   - `shortNote`: Concise 1–2 sentence high-yield definition displayed inside the Concept Card when clicked.
4. **Option-by-Option Elimination Breakdown (Pink)**:
   - `reference`: Exact Article, Section, Schedule, or Scientific Mechanism badge (e.g., `Article 393, Part XXII`).
   - `explanation`: Surgical statement (strictly capped at **25–35 words**) of *why it is right or what exact word makes it wrong*. Zero peripheral fluff.
5. **Forward-Looking Value Addition (Yellow)**: Structured Topic Micro-Cards (`[{ title, tag, bullets }]`) expanding on the adjacent high-yield facts of the entities in the question and distractor options.
6. **In-News Connection (Cyan)**: A real current affairs headline from *The Hindu*, *Indian Express*, or *PIB* with exact entity matching (`[Source | Month Year]`).
7. **Similar PYQ & Question Family (Light Green)**: Direct cross-reference to an earlier or later UPSC CSE/CDS question to build pattern recognition.
8. **High-Yield Visual Comparison Matrix**: A 4-column comparison table (`tableData: { headers, rows }`) for native platform and slide rendering.

## 4. Editorial Guidelines & Quality Checklists

1. **Strictly Lexical Keywords**: Only include genuine terms physically present in the question stem or options in the `keywords` array. Never add artificial meta-tags (`~~Majoritarian Trap~~`).
2. **25–35 Word Cap on Elimination**: Each option explanation in `optionBreakdown` must be strictly 1 to 2 sentences (25–35 words max) stating precisely what makes it right or falsifies it.
3. **Dedicated `reference` Key**: Always separate the legal / statutory / scientific source badge into `reference` (e.g., `"Article 393"`) and keep `explanation` clean and un-duplicated.
4. **No AI Fluff Rule**: Never write subjective generalizations (*"plays a crucial role"*, *"promotes sustainable development"*). Replace them with statutory acts, years, COP numbers, articles, sections, and criteria.
5. **Temporal Anchors**: Any dynamic data (Ramsar count, tiger reserve count, species IUCN status, GDP figures) **MUST** include `"as of [Year/Month]"`.
